# CybrLoop Website Brief (موجز موقع CybrLoop)

## Purpose

The website is a concise, single-page public introduction to CybrLoop. Visitors should understand who the team is, its four strategic directions, how it approaches its work, and how to get in touch.

CybrLoop is a cybersecurity team. It brings together enthusiasts and professionals to collaborate, develop expertise, share knowledge, and contribute to advancing cybersecurity and improving digital security. CybrLoop is not primarily a security services company, training center, or research institution.

## Approved Public Description

> CybrLoop is a cybersecurity team working to improve digital security through technical projects, research, knowledge sharing, and specialized security services.

Use the approved website copy as written. The official slogan is **Secure the Loop.**

## Strategic Workstreams

Represent these four directions with equal importance. They are not necessarily established departments or currently active offerings.

1. **Services** — Specialized security services focused on helping organizations identify risks, understand their impact, and improve security.
2. **Research & Projects** — Researching security problems, developing tools, and working on technical projects that offer practical value.
3. **Community & Education** — Sharing knowledge through talks, workshops, technical content, and collaboration with the cybersecurity community.
4. **Competitions & Challenges** — Participating in CTFs and technical challenges to build practical skills, exchange experience, and explore new approaches.

Do not invent a service or advertise paid assessments. Do not imply that planned research, events, projects, or competitions have already happened.

## Audience

The site should be useful to companies, product owners, developers, cybersecurity professionals, community and event organizers, and potential collaborators. No audience should make the four workstreams appear secondary.

## Single-Page Structure

1. Navigation
2. Hero — retain **Secure the Loop.**, introduce the whole team, and link to workstreams and contact.
3. Who We Are — technical backgrounds, collaboration, shared knowledge, and practical solutions.
4. What We Do — four equally weighted strategic workstreams.
5. Our Approach — Understand, Collaborate, Build, Share, and Improve. These are general principles, not a formal service delivery workflow.
6. Contact — inclusive invitation with WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/963982698204`) and Telegram (`https://t.me/cybrloop`) links.
7. Footer — official logo, short description, WhatsApp and Telegram, existing LinkedIn and GitHub links, copyright, and domain.

Research and community belong within the workstreams. Add a distinct section only when it contributes useful information without repeating them.

## Content Rules

Use direct, professional, technical, and collaborative language. Be clear about what is exploratory and what is available. Avoid fear-based marketing and unsupported security claims.

Do not invent clients, partnerships, team size, statistics, testimonials, certifications, published projects, company history, or operational services. Do not claim formal recruiting or acceptance of paid assessments.

## Visual and Technical Direction

Follow `docs/brand.md`: Structured Technical Minimalism, dark neutral surfaces, restrained Signal Orange, Geist typography, generous space, fine lines, technical geometry, and official CybrLoop assets. Preserve the existing responsive behavior, subtle animation, and accessibility patterns. Avoid generic cybersecurity imagery and unnecessary dependencies.

Use Next.js App Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Prefer static rendering and Server Components. Keep this a small, fast, accessible single-page website.

## Metadata

Canonical domain: `https://cybrloop.com`.

Metadata and social previews should describe CybrLoop as a cybersecurity team across its full range of interests. Do not reuse service-first copy in titles, descriptions, structured data, or Open Graph imagery.

## Validation

Run lint, TypeScript checking, and the production build. Review desktop and mobile layout, anchors, CTA links, contrast, spacing, and content alignment with the approved description and `docs/brand.md`.
